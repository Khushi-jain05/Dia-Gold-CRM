# Recording the next call (T-11)

11½ minutes of the 11 September call have no audio: Meet was playing through
Bluetooth earbuds (**NOISE EARBUDS**), which a screen recording does not
capture. The active-speaker ring proves the conversation happened; none of it
survived. Before the next call:

1. **Before joining** — Meet ▸ Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Speakers = *MacBook Air
   Speakers*. Not the earbuds. Check it again after joining (Meet remembers
   the last device).
2. **Test** — start the recording, have someone on the call say a sentence,
   stop, play it back. 10 seconds. Only then let the client in.
3. **Backup** — turn on Meet captions (and the transcript if the account has
   it). Captions are free insurance against the same failure.
4. **If earbuds are needed for privacy** — use a recorder that captures
   application audio (QuickTime cannot; OBS with a system-audio source, or
   Meet's own recording if the account allows it).
5. **After the call** — check the file plays with the client's voice before
   closing the laptop.

The same applies to the factory session, which will be longer and noisier.
