# Recording the next call (11 Sept T-11 · 18 Sept T-13)

11½ minutes of the 11 September call have no audio (Meet on Bluetooth
earbuds); the 18 September recording is at −45 dB and two windows with our
own questions are unrecoverable. Before the next call, in this order:

0. **Prefer Meet's own "Record meeting"** (⋮ ▸ Manage recording) if the
   account allows it — it captures every participant at full level and
   produces a transcript. Local screen recording is the fallback.

1. **Before joining** — Meet ▸ Settings ▸ Audio ▸ Speakers = *MacBook Air
   Speakers*. Not the earbuds. Check it again after joining (Meet remembers
   the last device).
2. **Test — 30 seconds, with a teammate speaking from another device** —
   start the recording, play it back, check both voices are clearly audible
   at normal volume (not −45 dB). Only then let the client in.
3. **Backup** — turn on Meet captions (and the transcript if the account has
   it). Captions are free insurance against the same failure.
4. **If earbuds are needed for privacy** — use a recorder that captures
   application audio (QuickTime cannot; OBS with a system-audio source, or
   Meet's own recording if the account allows it).
5. **After the call** — check the file plays with the client's voice before
   closing the laptop.

The same applies to the factory session, which will be longer and noisier.
